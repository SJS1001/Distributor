## Exact boundary examined

`RestoreActivation.offlineReleaseHistoryInTransaction()` uses its existing Platform store and the application's existing writer transaction. It requires a raw `platform_recovery` singleton and preflights count/UTF-8 record size in SQLite. Bounds are 1,000 rows, 8 MiB per record and 64 MiB aggregate. Its complete result includes terminal and unresolved release histories, checks native record digest/version/head, delegates history-shape classification to the pure phase validator and freezes the detached projection.

The reader does not validate full dossiers, approvals, file identity, fresh external trust or generation establishment. Presence of the raw hold is its local precondition; the method neither returns nor independently qualifies the hold tuple. Copied terminal histories must remain visible after `isolateRestore()` replaces the raw recovery tuple. The tests do not invent a requirement to reject retained history merely because it belongs to the prior generation. A future coordinator must qualify the current generation separately. Historical classification, valid row hashes and the writer lock prove neither external isolation nor release authority.
