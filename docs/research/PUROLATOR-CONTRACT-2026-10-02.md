# Purolator E-Ship contract research — 2026-10-02

**Result: current implementable contract not obtained.** Public official pages establish the E-Ship Web Services (EWS) product and advertised capabilities, but this research obtained no current shipment request/response specification, supported API schema version, authentication contract or lost-response recovery guarantee. This is an access/specification prerequisite for D-027 and D-036, not implemented support, certification or a verified product gate.

## Request, idempotency and recovery findings

The reviewed official public pages do not establish any server-side idempotency key, uniqueness scope, retention window, duplicate-create behavior or safe automatic retry rule. They also do not establish a read-only booking lookup that can recover the exact shipment and label after a successful create whose response was lost.

The overview's reference-based tracking claim does not prove uniqueness, immediate visibility, authoritative shipment existence, access to original labels, or reliable absence. Likewise, tracking a known PIN cannot recover a PIN never received. An echoed request reference would need documented deduplication semantics before it could be treated as idempotency.

**Recommendation:** keep Purolator protocol access disabled and do not invent request fixtures from another Purolator product or a third-party wrapper. Obtain the authorized current EWS package and written operation/recovery clarification first. Prefer one bounded initial scope: eligible Canada-origin ordinary parcels and approved Canada–US services, with exact account/service and customs constraints. Qualify US-origin, international, freight, dangerous goods and other special products separately. Preserve the existing coordinator's uncertain-booking hold and no-resend behavior unless provider evidence supports a safer transition. If authoritative recovery is unavailable, explicitly retain unsupported recovery and require independent operator/provider evidence to resolve an unknown outcome.

## Exact missing-specification checklist

The owner/provider handoff should supply these inputs without placing secrets or restricted vendor material in the public repository:

1. **Current authoritative package:** supported EWS API products/transports; dated version and revision; complete OpenAPI or WSDL/XSD imports; operation guides, changelog, retirement/migration notices and permission to use/store any material. Confirm the applicable CCS revision separately.
2. **Environment and authentication:** approved development and production base URLs; authentication/token/key header or SOAP-header formats; credential scope, expiry, rotation/revocation; test-account mapping; billing-account authorization; TLS and network restrictions. No secret values belong in research documents.
3. **Selected service contract:** exact origin/destination and service codes, account entitlements, shipment dates/cutoffs/time zones, currency, payer roles, dimensions/weight rounding and limits, parcel count, address rules, surcharges, declared value and prohibited/special-goods constraints.
4. **Rating and creation:** canonical required/optional fields; estimate lifetime and relation to charged price; validation versus booking effects; shipment/package identity; multi-piece partial success; error codes and definitive-versus-uncertain failure classification; timeouts, throttling, maintenance behavior and retry guidance.
5. **Duplicate prevention:** whether create supports provider-enforced idempotency; field/header, uniqueness scope, retention, payload-conflict behavior, replay response, concurrent duplicate handling and behavior after process/network failure. Obtain an explicit unsupported answer if absent.
6. **Lost-response recovery:** exact read-only operation; searchable client reference and uniqueness scope; propagation delay, retention and pagination; proof tying account, service, address, packages and native request to provider shipment identity; retrieval of the original label/documents; authoritative absence semantics. Establish whether lookup/reprint itself has fees, limits or mutation effects.
7. **Lifecycle:** cancellation/void deadlines and post-handover behavior; cancellation idempotency/recovery; return-label and replacement rules; manifests/closeout dependencies and retry semantics; label formats, validity, reprint limits and document storage/expiry. Confirm tracking/webhook availability, authentication, duplicate/out-of-order events and retention.
8. **Cross-border and acceptance:** customs schemas and required identities, broker/duty terms, service/account eligibility; current sandbox test cases and expected results; application-specific production activation/certification process and ongoing recertification. Obtain actual CA/US disclosure evidence for processing locations, subprocessors, retention/deletion and customer permission before any approved provider traffic.

After those inputs arrive, implement a disabled-by-default client against the confirmed package, record synthetic protocol/recovery checks against its exact revision, and keep actual account/service, certification, label/device and residency qualification separate.
