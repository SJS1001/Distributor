# Provider, residency and device plan

2026-09-30. Owner targets: US/Canada, Stripe, QuickBooks, major carriers/devices and customer choice of residency. All provider, hardware and product gates remain NOT VERIFIED.

## Customer choice

Organizations use their declared CA or US application region. Accounts start with strict regional processing. Customers may explicitly choose named Stripe/QuickBooks exceptions; acceptance is versioned and audited. Buyers may change their own choice; authorized staff may record it for an account. A staff acknowledgment alone is not independent evidence of customer consent.

The current choice is checked before intent creation, sends, reconciliation and payment retrieval. Withdrawal blocks subsequent requests; it cannot recall already transmitted data or in-flight requests. Native stock/orders/invoices and manual payment recording remain available. Changing the storage region requires an approved and verified migration.

Final disclosures must identify purpose, minimum fields, reviewed processing countries, subprocessors, retention/deletion and withdrawal consequences. Retain the disclosure version with acceptance. [Stripe's DPA](https://stripe.com/en-ca/legal/dpa) permits US transfers and global processing. QuickBooks and carrier processing locations need applicable vendor evidence. Region flags do not prove infrastructure, backup/log/support or processor residency.

Current carrier consent is a placeholder family `carrier`; no carrier adapter sends data. Replace it with individually named choices before enabling carriers. An umbrella exception must not silently authorize every vendor.

## Current executable provider boundary

Environment configuration supports one trusted organization binding. Use separate CA/US stores and runtimes. Binding ID, organization ID and current active staff finance principal are required; the worker re-reads regional organization and grants. Finance/support may send/reconcile effects; settlement and callback retry require finance. Admin includes these grants.

Providers default off. Scripts read exported environment variables, not `.env.example` automatically. Stripe accepts only test credentials and rejects live/connected-account events. QuickBooks uses its sandbox host and a short-lived environment token; durable OAuth/refresh/revocation and rotation remain pending. Local tests made no actual provider requests.

Following approved sandbox setup and customer choice, `npm start` serves HTTP and `npm run worker` processes one foreground batch then exits. It considers up to 20 pending effects, 20 unresolved refund polls, 20 refund callbacks and 20 checkout callbacks per binding. These separate batch limits can total 80 operations, with additional provider validation reads per operation. No schedule or background automation is installed. Keep provider access disabled for synthetic tests.

## Recovery and continuation

Sends move pending → running → completed, or unknown after lost responses. Each checkout/accounting send or reconciliation read acquires an exclusive durable token. Recovery invalidates tokens abandoned for two minutes and makes abandoned sends unknown; a stale sender/read cannot bind a result, overwrite an absence, or release its successor's claim. Recovery is scoped to the configured organization and also clears interrupted read claims. Legacy running intents without tokens still become unknown. Unknown operations are never automatically resent. Stripe lookup examines at most 1,000 sessions from the creation window; absence is inconclusive. QuickBooks accepts one matching invoice or cash payment; multiple results require review.

Current active finance/support grants and credential-change status are checked inside claim and completion transactions. Checkout/accounting queue commands recheck current grants, restore clearance and named customer consent before returning cached intents. Settlement requires current finance authority before and after retrieval, including inside its cash transaction; a restore hold prevents binding or cash application after I/O. Consent withdrawal during an already authorized call cannot recall its response: its qualified outcome may be retained, while subsequent outbound reads remain blocked.

Callbacks move pending → processing → completed/waiting/blocked/failed. Waiting reads become due after 30 seconds; interrupted attempts become waiting after two minutes. Attempt numbers prevent abandoned workers overwriting successors. Residency/grant restrictions block verification; identity/money conflicts need finance review. UI/worker errors omit provider bodies, secrets and stack traces.

Operators use “Provider operations” and “Payment confirmations,” reconcile unknown sends and resolve causes before retrying verification. Synthetic tests cover lost responses, duplicates, stale claims and a new database connection. Actual termination during provider IO, external retries, rotation, prolonged outages/backlogs and alerting remain unverified.

## Credited cash refunds

Finance first credits eligible invoice units, then requests a refund against one original payment. Pending/unknown reservations count against credited cash and that payment's unrefunded amount. Requests do not move stock or add a second credit. The Billing screen shows retained native requests and provider observations; manual payments require a separate unique bank repayment reference. Recording manual evidence is not independent proof of bank settlement.

For Stripe payments, finance queues one immutable refund intent, then sends the pending operation using its stable effect idempotency key. The test-only adapter retrieves the original settled PaymentIntent and verifies test mode, identity, received amount and currency before creating or reading a refund. The request sends only the payment identity, integer amount and internal effect/refund metadata. [Stripe creation documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/api/refunds/create) describes that interface; [refund objects](https://docs.stripe.com/api/refunds/object) have statuses `pending`, `requires_action`, `succeeded`, `failed` or `canceled` and do not themselves expose a live-mode flag.

Only a matching provider refund changes native cash. `succeeded` completes the native refund; `pending`/`requires_action` retain an unknown cash outcome and its reservation; `failed`/`canceled` reject it and release the reservation. Observations remain immutable, including stale statuses ignored after success or a terminal failure. A later bank failure can reverse an earlier completed refund while retaining both observations. A returned transfer may instead become `requires_action`, removing confirmed refunded cash and reserving it again until correction succeeds or a terminal failure releases it, consistent with [Stripe refund handling](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds). This accommodates provider observations; additional payment methods are not qualified by it and current checkout creation remains card-only. Operators must reconcile the bank/customer outcome before any new repayment.

A lost send response remains unknown and is never automatically resent. “Check provider outcome” reads the original intent. A known refund is retrieved by its exact provider identity. An unbound lookup examines at most ten pages of 100 refunds for the original payment and requires one matching effect marker; duplicates or an exhausted search require review. No match proves neither failure nor safe resend. See [Stripe refund list pagination](https://docs.stripe.com/api/refunds/list). A new request/key must not be used to bypass unknown-outcome recovery.

The bounded worker polls previously bound unresolved statuses at least 30 seconds apart. Unknown sends require explicit reconciliation or a matching signed refund receipt; succeeded/failed/canceled outcomes are not periodically polled. Finance can use “Refresh refund status” for later changes, including bank returns. The same raw-body webhook endpoint also accepts test `refund.created`, `refund.updated` and `refund.failed` events with Distributor effect/refund markers. It validates organization-scoped native identity, original PaymentIntent, amount/currency and any prior provider binding, then commits only minimal identifiers/hash and audit before acknowledgment. Unmarked manual refunds are ignored; partial/foreign markers fail closed. Manual Dashboard refunds need separate reconciliation.

A refund callback is a read hint: the worker retrieves that exact refund identity and validates the original settled test payment before applying current provider status. Delayed event status cannot override the retrieved outcome or cause another send. Unknown send outcomes can bind to this verified identity; a missing/mismatching result requires review and never permits resend. Callback completion, cash/observation/effect changes and audit commit together. Current grants/credential status, restore isolation, refund read tokens and callback attempt numbers fence revoked or abandoned workers. Withdrawn consent permits passive receipt capture but blocks the outbound read. Waiting errors are redacted with 30-second backoff; finance can retry resolved restrictions/conflicts in the combined confirmations table. Completion means verification processed, including a verified rejected refund.

Automatic failure alerts, customer requires-action instructions, webhook subscription/delivery configuration, actual Stripe/bank acceptance and real customer payment/refund browser journeys remain pending. See the [refund event receipt](evidence/LOCAL-REFUND-EVENTS-2026-10-01.md) for synthetic signature/runtime/HTTP, atomic rollback and separate-process checks. No live or connected-account event is accepted; no actual provider request occurred.

Fresh active finance grants and regional identity are checked before and after I/O. Current named Stripe consent is required before queue-cache access and each outbound call. If consent is withdrawn during a call, its already received outcome may be retained; subsequent reads are blocked. A restored provider hold blocks calls and completion. A token fences abandoned send/read workers; the runtime clears leases older than two minutes and marks abandoned sends unknown, requiring a read. Matching money, permanent provider binding, native state, observations, effect and audit/event changes commit together.

## QuickBooks invoice and recorded cash handoffs

Billing exposes finance-only queue controls for native invoices and their recorded manual or verified Stripe cash. Queueing requires current active finance grants, credential status, restore clearance and the customer's current named QuickBooks permission, including cached retries. Support may send or reconcile queued operations but cannot create invoice/payment mappings. Buyers and commercial staff cannot view internal payment operations. Imported invoices remain blocked from new accounting posting until their existing ledger identities are reconciled.

First queue the native invoice with company-specific customer, item, tax code and tax rate IDs, then send/reconcile it to completion. A recorded payment requires that completed invoice's permanent external identity. The immutable payment intent freezes the native payment, original invoice, customer/currency, deposit account, applied integer cents and stable payment reference. Creating or completing it does not record native cash again, alter a credit/refund, or change orders, invoices or stock.

The operator explicitly chooses how much cash to apply; the dialog defaults to zero. The remainder stays unapplied in QuickBooks. [Intuit's Payment API](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/all-entities/payment) supports invoice applications and unapplied customer cash. The adapter posts an accounting record with `ProcessPayment: false`; it requests no new charge, consistent with the [SDK payment field documentation](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ippphpdevkitv3/entities/files/IPPPayment.html). Provider charges are not authorized by this handoff.

Before posting cash the adapter retrieves the exact QuickBooks invoice and checks its Distributor marker, original number, customer, currency, total and available balance. It then posts the original gross cash, explicit deposit mapping, UTC native recording date, stable reference and one invoice application (or none for wholly unapplied cash). Matching identity, gross/unapplied amounts, customer/currency/deposit/date and exact application are required before completion. Payment identities are entity-qualified to avoid collisions with invoice IDs. Reads query the stable reference with a two-result bound; duplicates require finance review, absence stays unknown, and no unknown send is automatically repeated.

Integration owns durable application reservations committed atomically with each intent. Pending, unknown and completed amounts together cannot exceed the original invoice total, including competing processes. Reservations are retained permanently; there is no cancellation/reallocation or automatic release after a provider error. This is a conservative local engineering rule pending approved finance policy. It is not a complete ledger reconciliation: native credits/refunds, external edits and imported payment identities need separate handling. The external balance read and subsequent post are not atomic against other QuickBooks actors; provider behavior under those races still needs qualification. The chosen deposit/currency/company mappings, UTC posting date, bank fees/settlement dates, accounting periods and tax behavior remain provisional.

The [local accounting cash receipt](evidence/LOCAL-ACCOUNTING-CASH-2026-10-01.md) covers strict HTTP commands, immutable retries/restart, late-write rollback, competing allocations, current permission/restore checks, mocked QuickBooks response contracts and the Billing queue/send/reconcile journey with lost responses. ## QuickBooks unapplied credit handoffs

After recording a native credit, finance can queue its QuickBooks handoff from Billing once the original invoice operation is completed. The command accepts only the native credit ID. Billing supplies original credited quantities, prices, tax and document totals; integration inherits customer/item/tax mappings and the permanent external invoice identity from the completed invoice intent. One immutable effect survives retries, restarts and competing command keys. Current finance authority, credential status, customer permission and restore clearance precede cached results. Support may send/read queued operations; buyers and commercial staff cannot see internal credit operations.

This creates an unapplied CreditMemo. It does not apply credit to an invoice, create a Payment or RefundReceipt, repay cash, change native stock, or record a second native credit. Those actions require separate reconciliation. Intuit exposes the credit's available amount as `RemainingCredit` in its [CreditMemo SDK fields](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ippphpdevkitv3/entities/files/IPPCreditMemo.html). The adapter requires that amount to equal the entire original gross credit before completion.

Before creation, the adapter reads company preferences and requires `SalesFormsPrefs.AutoApplyCredit` to be explicitly false. The [official preference fields](https://static.developer.intuit.com/sdkdocs/qbv3doc/ippdotnetdevkitv3/html/eb3307eb-ef7b-1962-72f0-6fac624dbb4d.htm) identify that setting as automatic credit application. Missing/enabled settings block creation; Distributor does not change company preferences. It then rereads the exact parent invoice and checks original identity, effect marker, number, customer, currency and gross amount. An external settings/edit race cannot be locked atomically; an applied or altered response remains unknown and requires finance review.

Creation uses the stable effect request ID, original document number, UTC native recording date, original sales lines and explicit original tax mapping. Internal reasons/references, cash data and linked transactions are omitted. Completion requires exact document identity/marker/date/customer/currency, gross/unapplied amounts, sales-line quantities/prices/net/item/tax codes and one exact tax line. Sales lines are compared as a multiset; one matching subtotal is allowed, other monetary adjustments are rejected. Reads query the document number with at most two results. Duplicates or differences require review; absence remains unknown and never permits another send. Bound IDs include a credit entity prefix.

QuickBooks inventory items may affect its external inventory independently; that accounting mapping must be reviewed before activation. Credit application, cash refund/cost handoffs, external edits and legacy document identities remain unresolved.
