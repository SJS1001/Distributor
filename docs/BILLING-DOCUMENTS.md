Use [implementation status](IMPLEMENTATION.md), [financial checkpoints](CHECKPOINTS.md), [local receipt](evidence/LOCAL-BILLING-DOCUMENTS-2026-09-30.md) and [dependency notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

## Issuance and original facts

Billing owns party profiles, immutable document facts, stored PDF renditions and prepared download receipts. Finance/administrator can update issuer/customer billing names, addresses, tax registrations and customer terms with a reason and expected profile version. Empty addresses/tax registrations and unknown terms remain explicit. Names and registrations are recorded evidence, not verification of a legal identity or tax policy. Customer terms accept 0–365 calendar days; issuer profiles cannot set terms.

New native invoices capture both identities, their versions, exact original quantities/unit net/unit tax, currency, issued date, customer terms and derived due date inside the invoice transaction. No retrospective profile change rewrites those facts. Issued credit notes inherit original invoice identities and carry original invoice number, credited quantities and reason/reference. Current payments, credits or refunds never alter original PDF amounts. Each facts record must reconcile original line net/tax totals to the original document total; its hash is verified on access.

Earlier native invoices cannot recover missing historical identities/terms. Their first download records current billing identities explicitly as reconstruction, leaves due dates unknown and never applies current terms retrospectively. Imported opening invoices retain original due date, gross/credited/paid/refunded/outstanding amounts and source/cutoff provenance. Their PDF states that it is a reconstructed opening invoice, not the original source PDF, and that original identities are unavailable. Imported historical credited amounts have no invented original credit-note identity. See [unpaid-document imports](IMPORTS.md).

## Authorized downloads

The Billing screen offers Download invoice PDF and Download credit PDF. The authenticated POST route is `/api/billing/documents/:kind/:documentId/pdf`, with an empty JSON object, current same-origin session/CSRF and an idempotency key. Finance, commercial, warranty and support readers (plus administrators) are organization-scoped; buyers are account-scoped. Current principal and document authority are checked before cached results. After asynchronous rendering the session is re-read; expired sessions, deactivated users or changed account/role cannot receive cached bytes.

Facts preparation commits first. Rendering occurs outside a database transaction. The final transaction chooses one stored rendition, verifies fact/byte hashes and atomically records a `prepared` receipt plus command/audit evidence. A late receipt write failure rolls back bytes and request evidence; immutable prepared facts remain for retry. Simultaneous requests use the first committed rendition. A repeated key reuses one receipt/identical bytes; a new key records another authorized request for the same bytes. Stored renderer/facts/content hashes and bytes persist across restart; corruption fails before delivery. The browser retains its request key until it receives and verifies the PDF MIME type, SHA-256 header and nonempty download receipt, so a lost committed response retries the original receipt.

Receipts record requested actor/time, account/document, safe ASCII filename, exact byte count/hash and state. They establish prepared bytes, not that a file was saved, read, emailed, delivered or accepted. History shows the latest 200 authorized requests; audit/command evidence remains persisted. PDF responses use private no-store headers. PDFs carry original values, explicit missing details, page numbers, facts hash and reconstructed-document qualifications. Static Noto Sans supports tested English/French text, wrapping and pagination; unsupported glyphs/control characters fail without a prepared download. Additional scripts/font coverage, large rendering workloads and signed file archival remain unqualified.

## Current aging and export

Account aging reads current native ledger balances, using UTC calendar days from immutable native due dates or original imported due dates. It shows not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90 and unknown due. Unknown legacy/unspecified due dates are not assigned an invented overdue age. Positive debt and negative credit balances remain separate; signed net, order holds and pending/unknown refunds appear independently. Pending refunds do not represent settled cash. This is a current observation with a timestamp, not an arbitrary historical statement or currency conversion.

Finance/administrator can export `/api/billing/aging.csv`. Monetary fields are integer cents with account currency and observation timestamp. String cells quote embedded characters and prefix spreadsheet formula/control-leading values. A platform-owned export command/audit preserves actor, observation, account IDs and CSV hash with state `prepared`; it does not claim delivery. Buyers see only their own aging/download history and cannot edit profiles or export organizational aging.

## Native customer inbox — local checkpoint 2026-10-01

Finance/administrator uses **Review and publish invoice/credit** on the Billing screen. The browser verifies and downloads the existing immutable PDF, then asks the operator to review the exact document and customer and supply a reason before **Publish to customer inbox**. The server requires that same operator's original prepared-download receipt, verifies the persisted facts/bytes and records one active publication per original document. It does not assert that a human actually opened or approved the PDF.

Only a current buyer for that customer account can download a publication from the inbox. Downloading records a `prepared` request and does not record receipt confirmation. After verifying the PDF MIME type, SHA-256 and download receipt, the browser displays **Confirm document receipt**; cancellation leaves no acknowledgment. **Confirm receipt** explicitly records the buyer, account, publication, exact content hash, their own prepared request, time and versioned statement: receipt does not imply payment or agreement with its contents. Staff cannot confirm for a buyer. Repeated requests deduplicate personal acknowledgment; each buyer confirms separately. Downloading an already personally confirmed publication offers the same PDF without another confirmation dialog.

Finance can withdraw an available publication with its current revision and a reason. The original invoice/credit, downloaded copies, prepared requests and existing confirmations remain. Current state and grants are checked before cached buyer downloads/confirmations. Republishing creates a new publication ID for the original bytes and requires a new publication-specific buyer download/confirmation. A lost publish response retried after withdrawal reports the withdrawn state. No withdrawal changes a financial balance, stock or shipment.

The compatibility endpoint `/api/billing/inbox` retains its historical limit of 200 publications, with up to 200 requests/confirmations per publication; buyers see only their account and their own personal request/confirmation evidence, while authorized staff see organization evidence. Current paged APIs and UI controls are described below; production retention and operational scale remain pending. The [local inbox receipt](evidence/LOCAL-BILLING-INBOX-2026-10-01.md) covers domain/HTTP/process checks. The [browser receipt](evidence/LOCAL-BILLING-INBOX-BROWSER-2026-10-01.md) covers invoice/credit publication, cancellation, lost responses, missing receipt headers, personal/account isolation, withdrawal and republication through synthetic browser users. Human acceptance remains pending.

## Paged inbox APIs — local checkpoint 2026-10-01

`GET /api/billing/inbox/page` and `GET /api/billing/inbox/:publicationId/history/:kind` return `{items,next}`. History kinds are `downloads` and `acknowledgments`. Optional `limit` defaults to 50 and accepts integers 1–100; use `after=next` to continue. Publication pages include up to five recent personal/staff-scoped requests and confirmations plus their full counts. The current buyer's own unique confirmation remains visible. These routes are authenticated, current-grant checked and no-store. They do not change billing or stock.

Pages enumerate newest recorded insertions first and retain an initial upper insertion position across continuation, so later insertions and tied/backdated timestamps do not skip older evidence. Reload the first page to include newer records. Withdrawal retains readable authorized history. Cursors bind the actor, organization, role, account, publication and history kind where applicable. They are unsigned positional selectors, not authorization tokens: every query reapplies current scope. Revoked access fails; changed scope invalidates an old cursor. This is not a frozen snapshot of mutable publication state, counts or previews.

The [local API checkpoint receipt](evidence/LOCAL-INBOX-PAGING-2026-10-01.md) records domain/HTTP checks across more than 200 records, personal isolation, strict query validation and access changes. The subsequent [local UI checkpoint](evidence/LOCAL-INBOX-HISTORY-2026-10-01.md) records continuation/history controls and bounded browser checks for these routes. Physical history deletion, SQLite rowid reuse/VACUUM behavior, production indexing/load/retention, migration and operational recovery are unqualified. All tasks/gates remain NOT VERIFIED.

## Inbox continuation and history controls — local checkpoint 2026-10-01

The Billing screen loads the first 50 scoped publications and offers **Load older documents** while earlier records remain. It shows the number loaded, current publication availability when read, up to five newest confirmation previews and the full scoped confirmation count. **View document history** opens separate paged prepared-PDF requests and receipt confirmations, including withdrawn publications. Buyers see only their own evidence; authorized staff see customer evidence. Request records state prepared only; confirmation records retain the original statement and request/hash links. These records do not prove file saving, reading, payment or agreement.

A failed read leaves displayed rows and the continuation unchanged; **Retry older documents**, **Retry PDF requests** or **Retry receipt confirmations** retries the same page. Refresh returns to the newest first page and closes history. Closing/changing history, leaving Billing, refreshing or signing out cancels that view's pending reads; old responses cannot populate a replacement view. Closing history restores focus to its opening button. Publication buttons use a current invoice/credit status projection independently of inbox pages; the server still enforces one active publication and current authority at mutation time.

The [local UI receipt](evidence/LOCAL-INBOX-HISTORY-2026-10-01.md) records actual synthetic browser continuation, retry, refresh, withdrawn history, colleague isolation, pending-read cancellation and unchanged financial/custody records. This is bounded direct workstation evidence, with local commits only and no runner jobs or remote publication. It does not establish production pagination/load/retention, row deletion/VACUUM safety, human delivery/wording approval or any product gate.


## Recorded cash payments and refund selection — local checkpoint 2026-10-01

The Billing screen loads recorded cash payments in pages of 20. **Load older payments** preserves loaded rows; a failed read offers **Retry older payments** at the same cursor. Refresh returns to the first page; navigation, refresh and sign-out cancel that list's reads. The final page returns focus to the heading. Each summary includes the original invoice number/currency, recorded cents, provider/reference and recorded time independently of the dashboard invoice window. QuickBooks handoff uses those original payment facts and still requires the existing reconciled invoice and current provider/customer permissions. No page read charges a customer or calls a provider.

**Request refund** opens an independently paged selector for that invoice's original payments. Its first payment is selected by default; **Load older invoice payments** reaches payments outside the cash-history window. Failed reads retain loaded options and the selected payment; **Retry invoice payments** retries the same page. The final page restores focus to the selector. Closing or signing out cancels dialog reads. The operator chooses the original payment and refund amount; the native refund command still enforces credited cash, original payment limits and existing reservations. Reading or cancelling does not reserve or repay cash.

`GET /api/billing/payments/page` and `GET /api/billing/invoices/:invoiceId/payments/page` accept only optional `after` (1–128 characters) and return `{items,next}`. Up to 20 whitelisted summaries are returned from a query that materializes at most 21 rows. Both routes and the compatibility list recheck current active organization/finance/support/password authority before payment reads. Invoice selection checks the scoped original invoice; continuation checks the cursor's organization and, when selected, invoice. Missing or foreign cursors share a generic error; all HTTP reads are authenticated and no-store.

Ordering is descending recorded time then ID. Later newer payments appear after Refresh; permitted older insertions can appear during continuation, so this is not a frozen snapshot. Compatibility payments and other dashboard collections remain unbounded. Returned-row limits do not qualify SQLite scan/sort/locking cost, production indexing/load/retention, history deletion, actual providers/cash reconciliation, residency or operator acceptance. See [local evidence](evidence/LOCAL-PAYMENT-PAGES-2026-10-01.md). All tasks/gates remain NOT VERIFIED.
