# Move stock between bins

Warehouse staff and administrators can move a present, unreserved stock record to another bin in its current warehouse. This moves the entire serialized unit or bulk lot. The stock identity, quantity, original unit cost, condition, warehouse and supplier receipt lineage remain unchanged. For another warehouse, use the existing transfer procedure.

In Inventory, find the stock record and choose **Move to bin**. Physically check its source location, enter or scan **Confirm current bin** and **Destination bin in this warehouse**, and scan the exact serial for serialized equipment. Bulk lots have no serial field. Record the reason/evidence, then choose **Confirm bin move**. Cancel abandons the unsubmitted review. Quarantine and damaged stock retain their condition and cannot become available merely by moving.

The server rechecks current staff identity, password restrictions and warehouse grants. A new move requires the reviewed revision and source bin, a different nonempty destination, positive present stock, no sales or replacement reservation, and no submitted missing-serial review. Reservations include picked stock. Resolve holds through their owning workflow; stale reviews require refreshing and physically reviewing stock again.

Each successful command changes only the stock bin/revision and atomically retains a zero-quantity `relocation` movement, original cost, before/after bin evidence, actor/reason audit, cost sequence, local event and exact command result. Inventory cost reconciliation recognizes this movement as zero value; it does not create a money posting or change another module's records. A bin change makes earlier stock-count cutoffs stale.

If the response is lost while the dialog remains open, submit the same values to recover the original request key/result. Reusing that key with different data conflicts. A new key with the old reviewed revision cannot repeat the move. The native receipt survives restart. The generic browser dialog does not restore its submitted payload after navigation, reload, sign-out or storage loss; its session key alone is insufficient to reconstruct a review. Refresh stock and inspect history before deciding further physical work. A historical recovered result does not assert the stock is still in that bin.

Bins are presently trimmed text identifiers, not a validated warehouse bin directory or qualified location-label system. This operation does not split bulk lots, move reserved stock or establish physical custody from typed evidence. Partial lot relocation, bin configuration/capacity, physical devices/labels, real operator procedures and regional production infrastructure still need qualification. Local synthetic checks are recorded in [the engineering receipt](evidence/LOCAL-BIN-MOVES-2026-10-02.md). All product tasks and gates remain NOT VERIFIED.
