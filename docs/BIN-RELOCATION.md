# Move stock between bins

Warehouse staff and administrators can move a present, unreserved serialized unit or all or part of a bulk lot to another bin in its current warehouse. Original unit cost, condition, warehouse and supplier receipt lineage remain unchanged. A partial bulk move creates a separate stock record for the moved quantity and leaves the remainder in the original bin. For another warehouse, use the existing transfer procedure.

In Inventory, find the stock record and choose **Move to bin**. Physically check its source location, enter or scan **Confirm current bin** and **Destination bin in this warehouse**, and scan the exact serial for serialized equipment. For bulk lots, review **Units to move**, which defaults to the whole quantity. Record the reason/evidence, then choose **Confirm bin move**. Cancel abandons the unsubmitted review. Quarantine and damaged stock retain their condition and cannot become available merely by moving.

The server rechecks current staff identity, password restrictions and warehouse grants. A new move requires the reviewed revision and source bin, a different nonempty destination, positive present stock, no sales or replacement reservation, and no submitted missing-serial review. Reservations include picked stock. Resolve holds through their owning workflow; stale reviews require refreshing and physically reviewing stock again.

Whole-record moves retain a zero-quantity `relocation` movement. Partial moves atomically decrement the source, create the destination lot and retain paired `relocation.split.out` and `relocation.split.in` movements with one relocation reference. The result identifies both lots and their revisions. Original cost, before/after bin evidence, actor/reason audit, cost sequences, local events and exact command result commit together. Supplier receipt traversal follows split evidence, including repeated splits. Organization quantity and value remain unchanged; no money posting or foreign-module write is created. Both whole and partial moves make earlier source stock-count cutoffs stale. Native callers that omit quantity retain the whole-record behavior.

Before transport, the browser saves the request key, exact submitted payload and reviewed stock description in local storage scoped to the organization and staff account. If the response is lost or cannot be confirmed, the dialog shows the original details without editable fields. Choose **Retry exact bin move** to recover that command. Cancel closes the dialog while retaining an already submitted attempt. After navigation, reload or signing back into the same account, Inventory offers **Review retained bin move**. Another staff account does not see this recovery control. Reusing a native key with different data conflicts; a new key with the old reviewed revision cannot repeat the move. A historical recovered result does not assert that stock is still in that bin: refresh Inventory and inspect history before further physical work.

Web Locks coordinate submissions from tabs in the same browser profile. A competing tab must recover the retained original move before starting another move; a held lock blocks transport. Missing Web Locks support, unreadable recovery evidence and failed local-storage writes block transport. Storage cleanup failure retains the attempt for exact retry. Native validation, serial, stock, state and revision refusals clear the uncommitted attempt; stale stock/state reviews require closing, refreshing and physically reviewing again. Authority refusals, key conflicts and unknown outcomes retain recovery evidence. Current native identity and warehouse permissions still govern every retry, including cached results.

The native receipt survives server restart, but browser recovery depends on this browser profile retaining local storage. Cleared, corrupted or unavailable storage cannot reconstruct the original submitted payload automatically. Reconcile current stock and history before clearing damaged evidence or deciding further physical work. Stored details include product/location/serial/reason evidence in browser local storage; sign-out retains them for the original account. Shared-device policy, retention, physical workstation residency and storage protection remain to qualify. This is not authoritative offline movement or coordination between separate devices.

Bins are presently trimmed text identifiers, not a validated warehouse bin directory or qualified location-label system. This operation does not merge lots, move reserved stock or establish physical custody from typed evidence. Bin configuration/capacity, physical devices/labels, real operator procedures and regional production infrastructure still need qualification. Historical whole-record checks are recorded in [the original receipt](evidence/LOCAL-BIN-MOVES-2026-10-02.md); current partial-move checks are recorded in [the partial-move receipt](evidence/LOCAL-PARTIAL-BIN-MOVES-2026-10-02.md). All product tasks and gates remain NOT VERIFIED.

The [downstream qualification receipt](evidence/LOCAL-PARTIAL-BIN-DOWNSTREAM-2026-10-02.md) adds CA/US native checks through repeated splits, partial warehouse arrivals, recorded loss/recovery, supplier handover and original-cost accounting. Descendants share the original receipt's return limit even after count gains or competing handovers from different lots. Current warehouse visibility and administrator authority govern recovered results; supplier-credit differences do not change physical stock value. These synthetic checks do not qualify physical custody, browser downstream journeys or production operation.

The [browser recovery receipt](evidence/LOCAL-BIN-RECOVERY-2026-10-02.md) records local checks of exact whole/partial moves, reload/sign-out/navigation recovery, competing tabs, storage refusals and malformed committed replies. It supersedes the earlier generic-dialog retention limitation without passing any product gate.
