# Incoming supply commitments

Local implementation for D-013/D-019, 2026-10-05. This extends customer backorders with durable commitments against unreceived purchase order lines. It does not change any task or product gate to VERIFIED.

Commercial staff and administrators choose an open customer order line, an open purchase line for the same product and warehouse, a quantity, a priority (1–999; 1 first), and a reason. A commitment cannot exceed uncovered customer demand or the purchase line's unreceived quantity less other pending commitments. Smaller quantities leave an explicit uncovered shortage. Equal priorities follow original commitment creation order. Priority changes affect future arrivals; they do not displace stock already held or reserved.

A promise of incoming goods does not create physical stock, an allocation, or pick authority. A native purchase receipt first creates stock and its receipt provenance, then assigns that exact arriving stock to pending commitments in priority order within the same transaction. Partial arrivals partially fulfill commitments. Any extra receipt quantity remains available under normal stock condition rules.

Incoming stock under quarantine receives an Inventory custody hold. That hold reduces available stock and prevents competing allocation, stock reduction below the hold, transfer, replacement reservation, and supplier return from consuming the earmark. An approved stock inspection converts usable held quantities to ordinary physical order reservations atomically. Failed inspection to damaged releases the earmark and exposes the resulting customer shortage. A supplier return needs commercial release or customer cancellation first; it never silently steals an earmark. Bulk receipt layers may have holds for multiple customers, and inspection applies to the whole stock record.

Account credit holds and overdue reservation deadlines keep even usable arriving stock held. Resolve the account/deadline and use the commercial **reconcile** command to convert the usable quantity. Inspection also performs this reconciliation when permitted. A deadline expiry does not discard incoming commitments or custody holds automatically. They remain visible until explicitly released, canceled, reduced, or reconciled. There is no automatic reassignment to another customer.

Order reduction and cancellation remove excess incoming coverage, taking lower priorities and newer commitments first. Within each released commitment, pending supply is released before held goods. Previously converted quantities remain historical conversion counts; their ordinary physical reservations follow existing pick, cancellation, amendment and expiry controls. Generic physical allocation skips customer demand already covered by pending or held incoming supply.

Orders owns commitments and their action history; Inventory owns custody holds and physical allocations. Procurement calls the Orders arrival operation after recording the native receipt. Inventory calls the Orders inspection operation after changing condition. All are task-shaped module operations within the same database transaction; there are no foreign-table writes. `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, fresh order revisions, fresh role/account/site authorization, and actor-scoped idempotency receipts serialize competing commits and receipts. Cached command replay still checks current authorization.

The shared read contract is `src/shared/incoming-supply.ts`, returned by `GET /api/orders/:id/incoming-supply`. It includes order revision/state, demand and coverage per line, commitment pending/held/converted/released quantities, and open matching purchase candidates with remaining capacity. Buyer and warehouse readers retain their existing order scopes and receive no purchase candidate list. Commercial/admin candidates are not silently truncated. Commands are `order.incoming.commit`, `.release`, `.priority`, and `.reconcile` through the normal authenticated, CSRF-protected command endpoint. Every mutation supplies current order revision and a reason.

Schema 23 adds `orders_incoming_commitments`, `orders_incoming_history`, `orders_incoming_supply` (index), and `inventory_incoming_holds`. Existing schema 22 files require the explicit fresh-destination upgrade workflow; startup does not silently migrate them. Stop existing writers, inspect the source with `npm run schema -- inspect <source>`, and use the returned schema hash with `npm run schema -- upgrade <source> <fresh destination> <expected schema sha256> <CA|US>`. Activation remains a separate operator action. The upgrade preserves the source and existing business rows and creates empty incoming ownership tables.

Focused workstation evidence and limitations are recorded in the current handoff. Tests use synthetic customers, stock and users. This is not a production, load, operator or provider qualification. No deployment, provider I/O, CI or PR is required by this change.

## Native interface and exact-attempt recovery

Purchasing → Incoming allocations lists open customer orders; the same panel appears in order details. Commercial users can assign eligible purchase lines, set priority, release quantities and reconcile held receipts. Buyer/warehouse views retain scoped read access. Coverage shows usable allocations, pending incoming quantities, held receipts and uncovered demand.

Reviewed writes save the exact payload and idempotency key before transmission. An uncertain response remains recoverable after reload, including after authentication/authorization failures during retry. The focused browser journey verifies a server-committed/lost response followed by a 403 and exact retry, then partial release, at a 390-pixel viewport. This complements the native evidence in [the local receipt](evidence/LOCAL-INCOMING-SUPPLY-2026-10-05.md); it is not full-system acceptance.
