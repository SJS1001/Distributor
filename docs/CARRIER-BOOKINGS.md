# Reviewed carrier booking coordination

Local implementation, 2026-10-01. All 44 tasks and 10 product gates remain NOT VERIFIED; the full system is incomplete. No actual carrier adapter, account, service, request, label printing or physical handover is qualified by this checkpoint.

Warehouse staff with the current shipment site, or administrators under the existing administrator policy, can open **Review carrier booking** for a packed ordinary carrier shipment. Review the immutable packed destination and enter actual structured US/Canadian origin and destination, service, contact details and positive whole parcel dimensions in millimetres and weight in grams. No carrier is preselected. Confirm that the destination matches the packed address and the origin belongs to this warehouse. Named current customer acceptance and recorded disclosures are required separately. Preparation creates no external request, stock movement, cash or invoice.

Only one uncanceled booking may exist for a shipment. An unsent pending booking may be canceled with the exact review hash and a reason; its record remains in history. A successor preparation must identify the exact latest canceled booking. Pending, running and unknown bookings block manual handover and packing void. Cancel an unsent pending booking before using manual fallback. Unknown outcomes must be looked up; never resend or create another booking. A confirmed booking requires its exact carrier identifier and tracking for separate physical handover. Booked packing cannot be voided until carrier voiding is qualified. Booking itself does not ship stock or issue an invoice.

## Default-disabled provider boundary

`CarrierRuntime` accepts explicitly selected named sandbox adapters with unique organization/provider pairs. The normal server and worker create no carrier binding, no environment switch enables one, and no runtime credentials are supplied. The original `UpsSandbox` and `FedexSandbox` protocol clients are available for explicit trusted injection; they are not bound by normal startup. HTTP send/reconciliation remains disabled without an injected binding. A customer choice grants eligibility only; it does not configure or certify an adapter. Synthetic test adapters are used only in disposable local fixtures.

## Named runtime registrations

Trusted server construction may explicitly register multiple named sandbox carriers for one organization. Each organization/carrier pair must be unique; the same carrier may be registered separately for different organizations. Registration is separate from the customer's named disclosure acceptance. It neither authorizes an undisclosed provider nor grants shipment-site access.

Send and reconciliation resolve the provider from the retained booking through the integration owner after current role/password/organization/site and review-integrity checks. The runtime selects that exact organization/provider pair, regardless of registration order. A missing pair rejects without calling another adapter or changing the booking. HTTP actions continue to accept an empty object only; callers cannot supply a dispatch override. Unknown outcomes retain their original provider and reconciliation never authorizes another purchase.

Registration identity and method handles are captured at construction; mutating the caller's array, binding or method property does not reroute an existing runtime. Adapter methods retain their original receiver. These are trusted code bindings, not an isolation mechanism for untrusted adapters or a credential-lifecycle implementation. Normal startup still supplies no binding, and UPS/FedEx have original protocol clients with synthetic coverage; the other named protocols remain to be implemented and all actual providers remain to be qualified. See the [local routing receipt](evidence/LOCAL-CARRIER-ROUTING-2026-10-01.md).

A trusted adapter implements `book(intent, beforeWrite)` and read-only `lookup(intent)`. It must call the synchronous guard immediately before its sole write, after preliminary reads, and must not retry writes. The guard repeats current active warehouse/password/site authority, customer acceptance, restore clearance, account credit clearance, exclusive durable claim and unchanged native packed facts. The adapter receives a deeply frozen intent. Returns must identify the exact booking and review hash with canonical reference/tracking and bounded PDF/PNG/GIF bytes; external label URLs and unsupported result fields are rejected. File signature and SHA-256 checks establish byte integrity only, not valid rendering, malware safety, carrier acceptance or physical print readability.

Shared SQLite transactions claim execution and fence abandoned responses. A failed or uncertain operation becomes unknown, including a blocked pre-write operation: operator recovery requires read-only lookup, never a resend. `recoverStale(ageMs, orgId?)` fences stale claims; the default age is two minutes. This is an internal operation, with no automatic carrier worker or public recovery endpoint in this checkpoint. Production timing, process termination, monitoring and recovery procedures require qualification. After an authorized operation, a matching result can be retained if consent or credit changed during I/O; later processing remains subject to current policy. Revoked principal/site/password authority prevents completion and leaves a recoverable unknown outcome.

## UPS sandbox protocol client

`src/server/ups-sandbox.ts` implements an original UPS CIE client using the official [shipping/label recovery schema](https://github.com/UPS-API/api-documentation/blob/main/Shipping.yaml), [tracking schema](https://github.com/UPS-API/api-documentation/blob/main/Tracking.yaml) and [client credential schema](https://github.com/UPS-API/api-documentation/blob/main/OAuthClientCredentials.yaml). No SDK/vendor implementation or sample label is copied. Trusted configuration selects one organization, client credentials, account, registered shipper contact and exact reviewed service mappings. No browser/environment/startup binding or credential lifecycle is supplied by this checkpoint; do not provision actual credentials or activate requests without authorization and qualification.

The client supports a single ordinary parcel, domestic US or domestic Canada within the configured shipper country. The registered account contact is distinct from the reviewed physical warehouse origin. Positive integer grams/millimeters map to KGS/CM decimal strings. Local conservative caps are 68 kg, 2740 mm longest side and 4000 mm length plus girth; actual service/account/address/parcel eligibility remains unqualified. Customs, cross-border and multiple parcels require further implementation/qualification.

OAuth precedes the synchronous native write guard. Exactly one shipping POST is allowed per send; redirects and automatic retries are prohibited. A 35-character opaque reference binds organization/account/booking/review. This reference is correlation, not provider write idempotency. Bounded successful JSON must identify one canonical UPS tracking number and matching shipment number/context, with canonical base64 GIF bytes. Credentials/provider error text are never returned to the operator.

Unknown-outcome lookup authenticates, performs GET tracking by exact reference/account/destination and requires one shipment/parcel with the exact returned reference. Read-only label recovery by that reference must return the same tracking number, matching context and one label, without candidates. It requests no email/URL delivery, purchase or void. The tracking schema defaults to a 14-day search window; absent, expired, ambiguous or inconsistent results remain unknown, never authorize another purchase and require operator/provider investigation. This is a proposed correlation protocol tested against synthetic responses, not proof that a real UPS account/sandbox exposes or echoes these fields. Tracking reads for recovery do not record native delivery observations.

Private GIF downloads retain exact bounded bytes and hash metadata. GIF header/dimensions/trailer checks establish limited structural integrity only; no decoder, rendering, malware or print qualification is claimed. See the [local UPS receipt](evidence/LOCAL-UPS-SANDBOX-2026-10-01.md). Actual provider access/terms, credential lifecycle, field behavior, real recovery, service fees, devices and regional processing remain open.

## FedEx sandbox protocol client

`src/server/fedex-sandbox.ts` adds an original, explicitly injected client based on the official [Ship schema](https://developer.fedex.com/wirc/json/api_groups/Ship/Shipment-Resource.json) and [authorization schema](https://developer.fedex.com/wirc/json/api_groups/APIAuthorization/APIAuthorization-Resource.json). No vendor SDK/source/sample is imported. Trusted configuration selects one organization, nine-digit account, sandbox client credentials, country, existing pickup arrangement and exact ground service/residential mapping. Normal startup supplies no binding or credentials.

The reviewed physical warehouse origin is the shipment sender. The local client supports one domestic US/Canadian parcel with bounded ASCII contacts and a ten-digit phone, optionally prefixed by 1. US residential destinations require an explicit Home Delivery mapping; Canadian ground may be residential. Sender payment and either drop-off or an already scheduled pickup are explicit. No pickup is scheduled by this client. Positive whole grams map to kilograms; millimeters round upwards to whole centimeters. Conservative local limits are 68 kg, 274 cm longest side and 400 cm length plus girth after rounding; actual account/service/address eligibility remains unqualified. Omitted shipment date uses provider behavior, which still needs qualification.

Authentication precedes the sole synchronous native write guard and shipping POST. There are no redirects, write retries or token cache. A 30-character opaque parcel reference and transaction header bind organization/account/booking/review without native identifiers. They are correlation, not write idempotency. Bounded results require the exact returned transaction, service, one shipment/parcel, tracking, sequence and customer reference, plus one canonical base64 inline PDF for that tracking. No external label URL is followed. Private downloads retain bytes/hash after current access checks; the synthetic authored PDF is parsed in the HTTP test. Signature/hash checks do not prove real label rendering, safety or printing.

**Lost synchronous responses cannot currently be reconciled by this client.** `lookup` validates the saved review, then reports `CARRIER_RECOVERY_UNSUPPORTED` without authentication or shipping calls. It never returns an absence that could authorize resend. The coordinator retains durable unknown state across restart and blocks new purchase, cancellation, replacement and manual handover. Documented asynchronous job results require a known job identity; no documented recovery of the original synchronous label has been established. Provider/operator reconciliation and a safe recovery protocol remain required before activation. There is no operator override that marks an uncertain booking resolved.
