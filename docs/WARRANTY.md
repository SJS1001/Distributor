# Warranty, replacement fulfillment and manual manufacturer cases

Local implementation checkpoint: 2026-10-01. Tasks D-030–D-033 and G6 remain NOT VERIFIED. This is a synthetic engineering workflow; actual coverage, manufacturer terms, returned custody, financial policy and operator acceptance require qualification. See [proposed contracts](CONTRACTS.md), [decisions](DECISIONS.md) and [the local receipt](evidence/LOCAL-MANUFACTURER-CASES-2026-10-01.md).

## Native return workflow

A claim references a sold serial, customer, shipment and original invoice. Staff or the scoped buyer submit issue and evidence references; a warranty reviewer or administrator approves/rejects the claim with a reason. Current coverage dates use the organization's provisional coverage-days setting. A calculated date does not establish coverage eligibility or approved expiry rules.

## Sold serial coverage lookup

In Returns, **Check sold serial coverage** opens an on-demand panel. Select a currently sold serial and use **Check coverage dates**. Warranty/commercial staff, administrators and the current scoped buyer can inspect the original shipment time, calculated coverage end, assessment time and whether the assessment is before the shipment, within the dates or at/after the end. Every read rechecks the actual active identity, role, password-change requirement and customer account inside one SQLite transaction. Replacement handover retains the claim's captured original end and original shipment trigger; a later ordinary resale establishes the new customer's shipment/invoice. Reserved replacement stock is not sold entitlement.

An ordinary sale uses the current provisional organization duration, in whole UTC days after shipment. The engineering range is 0–36500 days; zero reaches the calculated end at the shipment instant. This is not an approved warranty term. Changing that duration affects the ordinary-sale lookup and future submissions, while existing claims and replacement dates stay retained. The lookup does not claim a historical policy snapshot. Replacement lookup does not consult the current provisional duration. Malformed durations, noncanonical retained UTC timestamps and out-of-range calculated dates reject lookup/submission before retained writes.

All results explicitly require eligibility review and report `coveragePolicyApproved: false`. The calculated interval uses an inclusive start and exclusive end solely for date presentation. Elapsed dates do not automatically reject claims or establish return eligibility. Actual coverage triggers, product/customer exceptions, expiry/ownership/transferability rules and policy-version approval remain unqualified. The panel fetches only on explicit request, clears prior results on serial changes/rechecks, permits retry, and cancels reads on close, refresh, navigation and sign-out. The sold-unit dashboard remains unbounded; production query cost, concurrency and operator acceptance remain open. See [local coverage evidence](evidence/LOCAL-WARRANTY-COVERAGE-2026-10-01.md) for the bounded checks and remaining limits.

An approved return receives the exact serial into quarantine at an authorized warehouse. Inspection records findings; a warranty reviewer or administrator selects repair, restock or scrap. Inventory owns the custody change. Finance separately issues the permitted original-invoice credit and records any authorized refund. Manufacturer acceptance does not trigger these tasks. Same-product serialized replacement reservation, customer collection and manually recorded carrier dispatch/tracking are available as described below. Actual carrier booking/labels, substitutions/procurement, physical manufacturer custody and approved coverage/expiry policies remain engineering and qualification gaps.

## Attach and download claim evidence

In Returns, **Evidence files** opens the selected claim's file panel. Authorized warranty, warehouse and commercial staff, administrators and scoped buyers may attach JPEG, PNG, PDF or UTF-8 text. Finance can read and download. Current real user identity, role, active status, password-change requirement, organization and buyer account are checked before every operation, including cached retries. Warehouse and warranty users need the claimed unit's current site grant; administrators bypass sites under the existing role model. Support has no evidence access.

Each file contains 1 byte to 5 MiB. The provisional engineering cap is 20 files per claim across both audiences. A required description is limited to 1,000 characters and the original filename to 120, without paths or control characters. Staff choose **Staff only** or **Visible to customer**, defaulting to staff only. Buyers upload customer-visible files and see only that audience; filenames, descriptions and cursors for staff files are excluded. Files retain their original bytes, SHA-256, description, audience and creation time. Same-key retries return the original result. Identical bytes/audience/metadata under a new key reuse the attachment; changed metadata on those bytes is refused for review. A different audience is a distinct attachment.

Lists contain metadata only in timestamp/ID pages of 10. Failed page reads retain already loaded rows. Downloads recheck stored size/hash/format and current authorization before returning bytes, including retries. The browser verifies size, SHA-256, media metadata, safe filename and download receipt before offering a file. Downloads use fixed identity-based names, attachment disposition, octet-stream, no-store, nosniff and sandbox headers. A recorded download request establishes prepared bytes, not proof the person saved or read them.

HTTP routes are `GET /api/warranty/claims/:claimId/evidence`, `POST` to that route for uploads, and `POST /api/warranty/claims/:claimId/evidence/:evidenceId/download` with an empty object. Both writes require an idempotency key, current session and origin/CSRF checks. Only the upload route permits the bounded base64 JSON body above the ordinary 256 KiB command limit. Existing manufacturer and inspection text references are independent fields; they are not automatically connected to a particular file.

[Local attachment evidence](evidence/LOCAL-WARRANTY-EVIDENCE-2026-10-01.md) records synthetic engineering checks. Image/PDF validation checks signatures rather than full parsing or malware detection; no inline previews or automatic provider transmission occur. Aggregate storage quotas, quarantine/malware review, retention/legal deletion, corrections, streaming and production migration/load/locking/recovery/security/actual regional hosting and human acceptance remain unqualified. Backups include the database bytes; a local encrypted restore does not prove infrastructure residency. All tasks and gates remain NOT VERIFIED.

## Reserve a replacement for collection or dispatch

An inspected or repair-state claim without a credit or active replacement offers **Approve replacement** to a warranty reviewer or administrator. Select an available usable serial of the same product, the returned unit's eventual scrap/restock disposition, original coverage inheritance and an approval reason. Reasons are required and limited to 1,000 characters. This provisional engineering policy requires business approval; it does not establish coverage eligibility or that a repaired unit is fit for resale.

Reservation creates revision 1, holds that serial against ordinary orders, transfers and other stock operations, and leaves the returned serial in quarantine. It creates no new order, shipment, invoice, credit or payment. Current grants for both units' warehouses are required to reserve, cancel or collect, including cached retries. An administrator bypasses site restrictions under the existing role model. This workflow records collection; it does not book a carrier or procure manufacturer stock.

The Replacement history section retains old/new serials, inherited coverage date, state and revision. Staff also see approval/cancellation history and collection recipient/evidence. Buyers see only their account's serial/state/coverage projection, without staff reasons, recipient or evidence.

## Cancel or collect a reserved replacement

A warranty reviewer or administrator may **Cancel replacement** with the displayed revision and a reason. Cancellation releases the hold, retains history and leaves the returned unit quarantined. A fresh replacement or ordinary disposition/return credit can then be selected. Closing a dialog changes no custody.

A warehouse operator or administrator uses **Hand over replacement**, scans the exact held serial, and records the collection recipient (maximum 160 characters) and evidence reference (maximum 2,000). Successful collection transitions once to handed_over revision 2, removes the replacement from warehouse stock and atomically applies the approved scrap/restock disposition to the returned unit. The claim becomes disposed with replacement disposition. The warranty return-credit operation rejects this claim; general finance credits remain separate invoice-level operations and still require approved remedy/reconciliation policy.

Cancellation and collection compete on the same revision; a stale attempt rejects and requires refresh/review. Wrong serials, missing recipient/evidence and stale revisions leave stock and money unchanged. Lost committed responses retry with the original key and unchanged payload. Collection does not prove physical handover or authenticate the evidence reference.

The replacement's current sold custody links future claims to the original shipment/invoice and captured original coverage end date, including another replacement in that chain. A later ordinary sale after a return/restock establishes the new customer and new invoice instead; historical replacement ownership does not authorize the former buyer. Coverage dates do not reset and do not enforce approved expiry/transferability rules.

HTTP commands are `warranty.replacement.reserve`, `warranty.replacement.cancel` and `warranty.replacement.handover`. Exact field shapes, current session, origin/CSRF and idempotency checks apply. Inventory owns holds and custody; warranty owns approval, history and original coverage lineage. Shared transactions commit these facts with receipts/audit and roll them back together on failure. See [replacement evidence](evidence/LOCAL-WARRANTY-REPLACEMENTS-2026-10-01.md) for bounded synthetic concurrency, rollback, ownership and browser checks. These do not pass D-030–D-033 or G6.

## Dispatch a reserved replacement and record delivery observations

A warehouse operator or administrator uses **Dispatch replacement** on the reserved row. Scan the exact held serial and enter the delivery recipient, delivery address, carrier name, tracking reference and physical handover evidence. Recipient/carrier/tracking have 160-character limits; address/evidence have 2,000-character limits. The trimmed NFKC-normalized, case-insensitive carrier/tracking pair is permanently unique within the organization's replacement shipments. This provisional single-package rule excludes consolidated replacements sharing one tracking number, multiple packages and carrier reference reuse; those need a separately qualified model.

Successful dispatch atomically performs the same held-stock handover and returned-unit disposition as collection, disposes the claim as a replacement, and records shipping in_transit revision 1. Stock cost/custody and original invoice/coverage lineage remain under their owning modules. It creates no sale, invoice, credit, cash movement, carrier booking or label. A concurrent collection/cancellation/dispatch cannot consume the serial twice. An unchanged-key retry recovers the original result after a lost response or restart; changed payloads conflict. Validate actual handover before recording it.

**Record shipping outcome** appends an external observation of in_transit, delayed, lost or delivered with the displayed shipping revision, a distinct evidence reference, an evidence description and canonical UTC ISO timestamp (for example `2026-10-01T12:00:00.000Z`). Times cannot precede the previous observation or be in the future; equal times are ordered by revision. References are normalized and permanently unique per shipment; `dispatch` names the initial event. Stale revisions reject. Delayed or lost may later become in_transit or delivered. Delivered is terminal; this workflow has no correction or reopen operation. A subsequent equipment problem follows the ordinary claim/return process under the original entitlement. Approved correction and lost-replacement remedies remain to be designed.

Observations change no stock, claim disposition, orders, invoices or money. A reported loss does not restock a shipped unit, issue a refund or reserve another replacement. Staff must handle physical recovery, authorized finance decisions and any additional remedy through separately approved procedures. Text evidence is manually recorded and is not proof of carrier delivery, authentic communication or a link to a particular uploaded claim attachment.

Current actual identity, active status, password requirement, organization and role are checked before new operations and cached retries. Writes require warehouse authority and current site grants for both original and replacement units; administrators retain their role bypass. History reads permit warranty, warehouse, finance, commercial and scoped buyers. Warranty/warehouse readers need both sites; shipped replacement rows outside those grants are omitted from aggregate replacement views. Buyers see only their account's carrier, tracking, status, dates and public revision history, without recipient/address, approval reasons, actor identities or evidence references/text. Support has no shipping access.

**View shipping history** loads ascending revision pages of 20, retaining earlier observations when a later page fails. It offers the same page again for retry. Dashboard claim/replacement histories remain unpaged; only this separate shipping history is bounded. Commands are `warranty.replacement.dispatch` and `warranty.replacement.shipping.update`; the read route is `GET /api/warranty/replacements/:replacementId/shipping/history` with an optional positive integer `after`. Strict fields, session, origin/CSRF and idempotency checks apply to writes. Warranty owns shipping rows/history and shares the command transaction with inventory custody, claims, receipts and audit.

See [the local shipping receipt](evidence/LOCAL-WARRANTY-SHIPPING-2026-10-01.md) for synthetic backend/process/HTTP/restore and browser evidence. Actual individual carriers, provider terms/residency choices, booking/labels/tracking retrieval, delivery authenticity, physical devices, human remedy/coverage/finance acceptance, production migrations/index/load/retention/clock/fault/recovery/security and infrastructure residency remain unqualified. No CI runners or actual provider requests are used; all tasks and gates remain NOT VERIFIED.

## Record an external manufacturer referral

In Returns, an approved unfinished warranty claim offers **Record manufacturer referral**. This is available for native states approved, received, inspected or repair. Enter the manufacturer, its actual case reference, referral evidence reference and reason. The workflow records communication arranged outside Distributor; it does not send a request to any provider.

Only a warranty reviewer with the unit's current warehouse grant, or an administrator, may record a referral or response. Buyers cannot read internal manufacturer names, references, reasons or evidence; their claim projection has an empty manufacturer-case list. Authorized warranty, commercial and finance staff can read organization claim history; warehouse staff are limited to their granted unit sites. These permissions follow the current local role model; human approval duties remain to be selected.

Manufacturer and reference are required, at most 160 characters each. Their trimmed NFKC-normalized, case-insensitive pair is permanently unique within the organization. A reused pair is rejected even after a case closes. There can be only one pending manufacturer case per claim. A different reference may create a follow-up after the preceding case is accepted, denied or cancelled. Original cases and evidence remain visible.

## Record a response or cancellation

The Manufacturer case history section shows every case's state, revision and evidence history. A pending case offers **Record manufacturer response**. Select accepted, denied or cancelled and enter an actual response/cancellation evidence reference and reason. Evidence is limited to 2,000 characters; reasons to 1,000. This stores text references, not uploaded or independently authenticated attachments.

Referral creates pending revision 1. A final response creates revision 2 and retains the original referral evidence. Terminal decisions cannot be edited or reopened; an additional referral uses a new reference. Accepted means staff recorded manufacturer acceptance of that external case. It does not establish payment, coverage approval, customer replacement or manufacturer possession of equipment. A pending case may receive its final external response after the native claim has been disposed; this preserves independent communication history.

If another operator decides the case while a response dialog is open, the stale revision is rejected. Cancel the dialog, refresh and review the actual history before taking another action. Do not relabel a conflicting response as a new successful decision.

## Claim activity

Returns offers **Claim activity** for each accessible claim. It loads submission, receipt, inspection, review, manufacturer and remedy records on demand in pages of 20, in retained recording order. New submission records preserve the validated issue (up to 2,000 characters); receipt records preserve the serial, receiving warehouse ID, validated bin and quarantine custody at that moment; inspection records preserve the validated findings (up to 2,000 characters). Decision reasons retain their 1,000-character limit. Later custody/remedy changes do not rewrite these snapshots.

Each new activity record commits in the same transaction as the native command, claim/stock changes, permanent receipt and audit. A late failure rolls them all back, including audit ordering. Exact-key retries do not append duplicates. New submission/receipt/inspection audit details contain only the activity ID; existing decision audits retain their reason semantics. Evidence references/files, manufacturer communications and replacement shipping observations have separate records.

Older claims may have gaps. Neither reads, startup nor cached successful commands fabricate missing submission/receipt/inspection entries. Subsequent authorized native operations append their actual activity. An empty panel means no retained activity, not that the claim never existed or was received.

Staff see recorded detail and actor ID. Buyers see only activity names, IDs and dates for their current account; buyer SQL excludes issue, findings, bin and actor details. Long staff details wrap within the horizontally scrollable table. Each read checks current identity, organization, role, password restrictions, claim account and applicable warehouse grant before looking up a cursor. Support has no access. Staff organization-wide review access follows the existing role policy; warehouse readers follow the returned unit's current site. Activity IDs are scoped to the same organization and claim; invalid/unavailable cursors reject instead of restarting traversal.

A failed page preserves loaded records and the same continuation for retry. Keyboard focus returns to the pager after retry or another page, and to the heading after completion. Closing restores focus to the opener. Switching claims starts a fresh list; closing, navigation, refresh and sign-out cancel pending browser reads. Refresh loads the dashboard once and completes before its button is enabled again. History reads change no stock, money, claim or communication facts.

The existing read route remains `GET /api/warranty/claims/:claimId/decisions` with optional `after` (an activity ID, at most 128 characters). Existing table, shared type, component and field names are retained for compatibility; staff `reason` carries activity detail. Unknown query fields reject. Pages contain at most 20 items and an opaque continuation or null. Recording order remains stable across tied timestamps and database restart. Later appends can appear on subsequent pages; this is not a snapshot traversal. No new schema or index is introduced: returned row bounds do not qualify scan, sort, lock or load cost. Dashboard claim/manufacturer collections remain unbounded, and retaining activity is not evidence of an approved business outcome.
