## Purolator: obtain the contract, keep a usable interim path

Purolator advertises E-Ship rating, labels, tracking and other services, but the public overview does not define an implementable API. The detailed [research note](research/PUROLATOR-CONTRACT-2026-10-02.md) records accessible official sources and failed access attempts. A blocked unauthenticated portal does not mean no API exists. The unrelated Mission Critical API and CCS software versions must not substitute for the E-Ship shipment contract. [Official E-Ship overview](https://www.purolator.com/en/services/technology-solutions/e-ship-web-services).

Recommend obtaining a dated official contract package through authorized customer/developer access: supported API family/version, schemas, test/production endpoints, authentication, rate limits, errors, shipment/void/manifest rules, label formats, reference uniqueness and retrieval after an uncertain create. Record document provenance and rights; keep secrets outside Git. Implement the verified contract family, rather than assuming REST supersedes SOAP or guessing payloads from samples.

Until qualified, recommend operators create Purolator shipments through their approved shipping tool and record the carrier/tracking reference using Distributor's existing manual fulfillment path. Keep the reviewed label in the approved shipping tool; this proposal does not claim a manual label-upload feature. Cancel any wholly unsent pending native booking first; running/unknown bookings cannot use manual fallback. This requires actual service, label and residency qualification; it is not automated Purolator support. A certified shipping intermediary is an alternative only after checking coverage, cost and processor disclosures. [Purolator certified providers](https://www.purolator.com/en/services/technology-solutions/e-ship-certified-providers).

For unknown shipment outcomes, hold for independent reconciliation. Only implement retry/recovery when the contract establishes what constitutes authoritative identity and outcome; no generic reference search or absent match proves that a shipment was never created. Synthetic fixtures can test timeout, duplicate response, stale authorization and unsupported recovery now; production binding and certification remain unavailable without the contract.

## Restore: reconcile before approving release

Recommend the following policy. NIST's contingency guidance supports validated reconstitution and defined responsibilities; it does not choose Distributor's cutoff, approvers or recovery targets. [NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/34/r1/upd1/final).

1. **Establish the source boundary.** Stop source writers, workers and callbacks and reconcile in-flight external operations before a planned snapshot. Record source identity, region, schema/profile, archive hash, freeze time and final durable business cursors for each module. Snapshot timestamps alone are insufficient. If the source was lost, record the last independently evidenced complete boundary, uncovered interval and potential data loss; keep unresolved outcomes on hold.
2. **Build a bounded reconciliation dossier.** Compare restored orders/invoices/payments, stock/custody, approved cost packets and provider attempts against independent source and provider records through the intended cutover boundary. Cover every organization in the restored store. Warehouse operators attest physical custody; finance attests money and ledger outcomes; security attests current grants, fresh secrets and residency. Unknown payments, labels or postings remain exceptions rather than becoming “not found” clearance.
3. **Use separate approvers.** Require distinct currently authorized finance and security release approvers, neither of whom prepared the dossier. Bind approvals to archive/source identity, candidate revision, reconciliation evidence hashes and policy version. Candidate writes, changed evidence, revoked grants or changed policy invalidate approval. Freeze the reconciled candidate before final approval.
4. **Prove one writer and route deliberately.** Operations supplies evidence that old application, worker, CLI and callback writers are fenced and routing targets only the candidate. A SQLite lock on a different database file cannot establish this. Persist the approved release transition atomically; interruption must leave a recoverable, explicit state. Provider readiness and per-provider unresolved holds still apply after store release.
5. **Define rollback by observed effects.** Before new writes or external effects, return to the verified source only after proving the candidate is stopped. After new writes, payments or labels, stop and reconcile the new interval, then recover forward or perform another controlled cutover. Never blindly restore the older snapshot or replay uncertain requests.

The existing restored store deliberately blocks provider IO. Recommend importing reports obtained through a separately authorized channel as offline evidence; building a qualified read-only reconciliation channel is a separate capability. Do not bypass the hold. Even a retained Stripe key is insufficient for indefinite recovery: Stripe permits pruning after at least 24 hours and treating reuse after pruning as a new request. [Stripe idempotency](https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests).

Proposed rehearsal targets: **RPO 15 minutes and RTO four hours**, configurable per installation. These are planning targets, not measured capability or a promise to discard unresolved data to meet a timer. Actual volume, backup frequency, staffing and residency must be tested. Synthetic acceptance should cover stale approvals, missing organization evidence, candidate changes, revoked approvers, competing releases, interruption/restart, unknown external outcomes and failed writer fencing. A local fixture cannot verify infrastructure routing or operator custody.

## Accounting: explicit valuation and append-only corrections

Recommend preserving the customer's established accounting basis and costing method. For a new deployment, propose specific identification for genuinely non-interchangeable equipment and FIFO receipt layers for interchangeable stock. A serial number alone does not establish non-interchangeability. Keep original acquisition/custody evidence; account for write-downs through separately approved valuation records. IAS 2 distinguishes these cost formulas and requires lower-of-cost/net-realisable-value measurement for IFRS reporting. This is a design reference, not a finding that every US/Canadian customer reports under IFRS. [IAS 2](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-2-inventories/).

Use a versioned organization/SKU policy with effective dates and approved opening layers. Defer moving-average support until its rounding, returns and correction rules are defined. Current original-unit-cost and receipt-order allocation does not itself prove complete financial valuation. Existing customers must not have their accounting/tax method changed automatically. [IRS Publication 538](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p538).

Recommend **Distributor as the inventory subledger**, with QuickBooks as the general ledger. Prefer qualified non-inventory sales-item mappings when Distributor owns stock-cost posting. Explicitly assign each receipt, supplier liability, sale/COGS, return, loss and valuation entry to one posting owner and account map. QuickBooks inventory-item sales can already change inventory assets/COGS, so parallel Distributor postings can duplicate costs. Intuit now documents both FIFO and MAC, with eligibility requiring confirmation for the actual country/edition/company; do not assume all QuickBooks installations support both. [Intuit inventory valuation](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/inventory-management/fifo-used-inventory-cost-accounting/L1x3hkunE_US_en_US).

Recommend monthly reporting periods with the customer's fiscal year and time zone, a local closed-through date, and no automatic period reopening. Corrections need a stated accounting classification and permitted posting date. Material prior-period errors may require retrospective restatement under IFRS, so “always post in the next open month” is insufficient. Escalate those cases to the responsible accountant with a separate reviewed restatement process. [IAS 8](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-8-basis-of-preparation-of-financial-statements/). QuickBooks administrators can change closing controls, so its password setting is not proof that Distributor's local history is immutable. [Intuit closed books](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/customer-company-settings/edit-closed-books/L76xHuaZ5_US_en_US).

For an approved stock-cost packet, preserve the original bytes/hash, cursor and permanent movement claims. Proposed correction states:

- **Verified unposted:** supersede with a linked replacement only after evidence that the receiver has canceled/quarantined the old packet and cannot later post it. Approval alone does not prove delivery or absence of posting.
- **Verified posted:** create a linked balanced reversal and corrected replacement in the explicitly approved period. Intuit's reversal operation creates a new entry with swapped debit/credit lines while retaining the original; its default date is not Distributor's approved correction date. [Intuit journal reversal](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/journal-entries/reverse-delete-journal-entry-quickbooks-online/L2prlrnkh_US_en_US).
- **Unknown posting outcome:** retain the hold until independently reconciled. No automatic reversal or replacement delivery.

Each chain must retain original/external ledger identities, reason, organization/region/currency, policy/mapping versions, posting date, exact balanced minor-unit amounts, separate preparer/reviewer and immutable approval evidence. Repeated identical requests return the same correction; conflicting requests cannot create another reversal. Resolve source stock errors through inventory-owned operations, rather than writing foreign tables or disguising quantity/cost errors as integration journals. Do not rewind the movement cursor to repost history.

Synthetic implementation checks can establish correction arithmetic, immutable history, duplicate/conflicting attempts, fresh authority, period closure and unknown-outcome refusal. Actual ledger acceptance, chart mappings, opening valuation, financial statement treatment and customer residency remain qualification requirements. These proposals change no task or gate status and do not implement a Purolator client, restore release or approved-cost correction workflow.
