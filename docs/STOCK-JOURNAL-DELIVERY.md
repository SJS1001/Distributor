Authenticated [review, queue and history APIs](STOCK-JOURNAL-CONTROLS.md) expose local task-shaped controls. No polling worker, transport registration or startup switch is connected. Creating an application never sends a journal. All tasks and product gates remain NOT VERIFIED.

## Review and identity

Preparation selects an approved original or correction source by exact hash, one unchanged posting date and leg, the current approved correction attempt if applicable, distinct receiver account mappings, the exact sandbox company and organization credential binding, current policy revision and exact [organization permission stamp](ORGANIZATION-LEDGER-RESIDENCY.md). Policy must assign Distributor inventory posting ownership. Closed-through dates refuse delivery; an explicitly unset date remains null. Source, mappings, policy and permission are frozen in a canonical reviewed plan. Mutable caller input cannot change it.

A separate current internal finance principal approves or rejects the exact review hash. Password-change requirements, current role/activity, organization scope, provider recovery hold, source supersession and permission are checked transactionally. Rejected selections remain in history and allow another review. Every other selection reserves its source/date/leg/attempt even if a later selection changes company or binding. Approval permanently reserves the actual 21-character QuickBooks document number, `DJ-` followed by 18 characters of the native journal-ID digest. Manual correction/retry references cannot reuse it. Receiver journal IDs are unique within organization/company.

## Exclusive operation ownership

An approved pending operation may acquire one 120-second write lease. The store retains its nonce, current principal, time and mode; only the issuing service's frozen lease object may invoke native fences. A second connection or process cannot acquire the same unexpired operation. Current policy, approved bytes, mapping, exact permission, role and hold are rechecked at claim, guard, synchronous final write fence and result retention. The write fence commits dispatch once with its audit record. Late audit failure rolls back both. No queue operation calls a provider.

Expired leases and clock rollback retain an unknown observation and clear ownership. Unknown operations may acquire read-only lookup leases; they cannot write again. Lookup misses, invalid/lost replies and withdrawn authority retain uncertainty. Local stopping remains possible after withdrawal, role removal, expiry or recovery hold; an old lease cannot release its successor. Lookup uses the retained approved bytes without imposing a newer write-period policy, but still requires the exact current organization permission and hold checks. Withdrawal/reacceptance changes that stamp and cannot automatically resume an old operation. [Separately reviewed permission replacement](STOCK-JOURNAL-PERMISSIONS.md) may bind the current choice through an independent append-only approval; uncertain or expired operations can receive lookup permission only.

Posted observations bind the exact company, source hash, leg, date, currency, balanced cent totals, receiver journal ID and sync token. They require a dispatched write lease or a lookup lease. History hashes bind organization, journal, revision, body, recorder and time. The detail read returns the newest 100 observations with an older-history flag; complete records remain stored and are available through separately paged, hash-checked observation reads. These are transport observations, not independent finance reconciliation. They do not mutate stock, the original artifact, manual cost acceptance or manual correction outcomes.

## Correction order and uncertainty

Replacement writes wait for the current reversal's native posted result if a native reversal exists. A manual posted claim cannot bypass its native uncertainty. A separately approved fresh correction attempt cannot write while its native predecessor remains unresolved. Cancellation of an unknown correction requires final cancelled/unposted operator evidence for that exact leg/attempt and an additional different finance reviewer linking its evidence hash to the actual native document number. The old reservation and observations remain permanently retained. Lookup absence supplies no cancellation authority.

Manual original acceptance and mapping-correction preparation cannot overtake a non-rejected native original. The bounded current contract requires one exact posted native original with matching company, receiver ID and date. Multiple-date source packets are not yet supported by that acceptance/correction bridge. Original cancellation/fresh retry, multiple-date reconciliation and further changed-journal/valuation correction chains require their own reviewed contracts.
