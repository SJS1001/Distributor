### Native maintenance authority is separately configured

`RestoreDossier.preparedBy` and external finance/security/operations signer IDs are external review identities. **None identifies or creates a native IAM actor.** They must never be cast to `Actor`, looked up as an implicit user ID, substituted into an administrator actor, or used to select a tenant. A valid signature grants no native projection permission.

Root must configure a default-empty, allowlisted mapping from `(organizationId, projectionName)` to a pre-existing native maintenance principal, with explicit permitted role and site scope. This mapping comes from trusted application/operator composition, never the dossier/manifest, claim payload or caller-supplied actor fields. No fallback to the ambient operator, first user, organization administrator or another organization's principal is permitted. Separate organization entries require deliberately configured authority even when operated by the same person.

Within the exact writer transaction, the identity owner resolves/revalidates that configured principal against the **requested organization**, current active status, staff/account restrictions, role, security/password policy and the complete relevant warehouse set. Then the native projection repeats its own permission checks. Integration uses its required finance authority; the carrier owner uses its required warehouse/site authority. A finance maintenance actor is not automatically eligible for carrier projection, and native admin convenience must not expand the explicitly configured projection scope. Missing configuration, missing/disabled principal, changed role/site/password requirement, tenant mismatch or unavailable authoritative identity mapping defaults closed.

Native IAM remains copied candidate state and is only one check: the recovery coordinator must independently validate current external maintenance authority and its explicit association with that configured local principal. This association is trusted configuration/current identity qualification, never equality between a signature ID and a database ID. It does not authorize creating or elevating that local user. A read-only projection receives only the narrow trusted receipt port it needs, injected by root composition on the same connection; it cannot supply its own port or receive a generic Platform store. These access rules apply to repeated/cached maintenance reads as well as newly evaluated ones.

## Exact task envelope and bindings

Proposed wire contract `OfflineTaskEnvelopeV1` has exactly the fields below. Required nested objects reject unknown keys. IDs are nonempty exact strings (no whitespace aliases, at most 160 characters); hashes are lowercase 64-character SHA-256; revisions/sequences are nonnegative safe integers; timestamps are exact UTC ISO instants. References/cursors may use the existing bounded 2,000-character evidence convention. Region is CA/US; currency is the actual organization's CAD/USD setting, never inferred conversion. Arrays reject duplicate identities and use documented canonical ordering before signing.

| Field                                                              | Exact meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `version`, `purpose`                                               | `1`, `distributor-restore-offline-task-v1`; cannot be accepted as a read-review or activation purpose.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `requestId`, `preparedBy`, `executorId`, `preparedAt`, `expiresAt` | Permanent request identity, externally established preparer/executor identities and bounded approval window. Both approvers differ from both preparer and executor.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `recovery`                                                         | `{instanceId, snapshotHash, restoredAt, sourceCompletedAt, schemaHash, region, organizations}`. `organizations` is the exact sorted list `{id,currency}` from the candidate, not just the task tenant.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `session`                                                          | `{id, revision, lineageHash}`. Lineage binds the prior offline receipts and any retired release IDs/bindings/control intents; authority never resets by losing a `current()` row.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `candidate`                                                        | `{logicalHash, file:{dev,ino}}` for the full pre-task committed candidate; path is operator-local configuration, not a signed substitute for identity. Existing schema hash and sidecar checks also apply.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `source`                                                           | `{identity, baseline:{logicalHash,durableCursor,auditSequence}, end:{logicalHash,durableCursor,auditSequence}, intervalEvidenceHash}`. The interval is baseline-exclusive/end-inclusive in the source's declared ordering. The end is an independently qualified stopped-source boundary. Empty intervals need explicit evidence. Opaque cursors are compared using the qualified source contract, never lexicographically. |
| `task`                                                             | `{name,version,owner,orgId,siteIds,subjectId,expectedRevision,expectedStateHash,payloadHash,priorClaim}`. `name` selects one statically registered task and exact payload schema; site set includes every affected custody site. `priorClaim` is null only if the owner's reviewed record proves no claim; otherwise `{operationId,tokenHash,revision,stateHash}` binds the copied claim without disclosing its token.      |
| `evidence`                                                         | `{setHash,items,qualificationHash}`. Each sorted item is `{reference,sha256,bytes}`; local manifest paths are separate. Qualification hash binds the independently authenticated issuer/report-scope/observation records described below.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `operations`                                                       | `{authorityId,revision,adapterIdentity,fenceTokenHash,observationHash}`. Binds the independently qualified fence and candidate maintenance lease, not an operator's self-declared booleans.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `trust`                                                            | `{authorityId,revision,registryHash}` from the current separately controlled registry; copied candidate IAM cannot supply it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

Task payload is separately carried and strictly parsed by its owner; `payloadHash = digest(canonical(parsedExactPayload))`. Unsupported properties fail before normalization, so different bodies cannot collapse onto one accepted request. The full `binding = digest(canonical(envelope))` is the signed subject. Finance/security approval messages are `canonical({purpose:"distributor-restore-offline-approval-v1",binding,signerId,role})`, with distinct current Ed25519 identities/keys. This is a proposed signature domain; existing review/release signatures do not substitute. Operations signs a separate attestation bound to that envelope's operational fields, candidate, source interval and task scope, with its own domain and trusted registry. Avoid a hash cycle: the observation/qualification records exclude the enclosing envelope binding; their hashes enter the envelope, and final finance/security signatures bind everything.

The operations authorizer must be independent of the preparer, executor and finance/security approvers. Machine signing keys identify a qualified service and accountable operator; distinct key strings alone do not prove distinct human duties. One exact task may affect one owning organization; a cross-owner or cross-organization plan is several separately scoped tasks with explicit dependencies and a final complete reconciliation. Do not infer a global negative outcome from one provider/account report.

## Phase assertion and transitions

Proposed internal assertion:

```ts
assertOfflineTaskInTransaction(
  currentActor: Actor,
  envelope: OfflineTaskEnvelopeV1,
  verified: VerifiedOfflineEvidence,
): OfflineTaskPermit
```

The permit is an opaque, nonserializable, in-process capability minted by the configured coordinator for this database connection, transaction identity, exact task/binding and expected session revision. The operator cannot pass `{verified:true}`, a receipt hash or a fabricated permit. It is single-use for a new effect. Its only consumer is the statically registered owning handler. This is a design signature, not a new exported production function.

The assertion must read raw hold and all relevant release/session history, require `Database.requireTransaction()`, refresh IAM and external authority, compare the entire envelope, enforce independent duties, and check qualified operations observation. Failure throws before any owner effect. A raw hold row **and** effective provider refusal are both necessary; neither is sufficient without the rest of this contract. Provider callbacks/workers, customer routes, token refresh, send/lookup and ordinary candidate writers remain externally fenced. The candidate permits only the named maintenance writer for the duration of this task; claiming that every candidate writer is stopped while this writer runs is incorrect.

| Retained state/history                                                                                                                                           | Offline owner mutation rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No raw recovery hold, malformed generation/history, wrong schema/file, or `permits()` true                                                                       | Refuse. Never create a hold as a side effect of an import request.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Raw active hold, no release control intent, no session                                                                                                           | Only proposed `beginOfflineReconciliation` may establish a session after current independently signed begin review and qualified isolation. No owner mutation yet. Beginning changes candidate bytes; task approval uses a newly captured pre-task candidate.                                                                                                 |
| Open reconciliation session, raw active hold, no unresolved release, approved exact task/current fence                                                           | Permit only that task inside its native transaction. A session flag alone grants nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Release `prepared`, with complete history proving only prepared revisions and no external control intent                                                         | Refuse imports. Root may implement a distinct CAS-guarded `retirePreparedRelease` retaining history, proving no control was issued, and invalidating all old signatures. Then begin a new signed session and capture again. Existing `supersede()` does not support this state.                                                                               |
| Any committed `fencing` intent or later intent, including `held` with an earlier phase, `fenced`, `routing`, `released`, `stopping`, `returning`, `forward-held` | Refuse imports even if a callback failed, runtime permission is now false, or no success was recorded. Uncertainty is not absence of effects. Require qualified safety stop, settlement of delayed controls, isolated route, current source/candidate fences, preserved interval/effect evidence and durable supersession before fresh session/task approval. |
| `superseded` or `rolled-back`, so `current()` returns null                                                                                                       | Still inspect history. Rolled-back may route to a live source. Neither terminal label proves present isolation. A source that resumed needs a newly established stopped end and interval; old stop or old source hash cannot authorize imports.                                                                                                               |
| Session sealed/abandoned, prior copied session, changed generation or replaced candidate                                                                         | Refuse further tasks. New signed begin review is required; no reopening with an old permit. Restoring a backup rotates instance authority and invalidates copied permits/claims.                                                                                                                                                                              |

Define **first control intent** as the committed `fencing` transition, not the later routing success. Once crossed, it is monotonic history for that release and descendants. `isolate()` during backup restore may mark a copied release superseded but cannot prove external source controls settled. If current adapter observations cannot prove safe stop/supersession for an uncertain interval, keep the hold; implementing a stronger operations recovery boundary is a prerequisite, not an offline exception.

`sealOfflineReconciliation` is a separate signed task with no provider IO. It requires every planned native disposition accounted for, no unconsumed maintenance claim, a currently stopped candidate, independently qualified source fence, current trust and owner projections proving internal consistency. Unknown external outcomes or missing owners prevent a release-ready seal; retain an unresolved session. Sealing persists a terminal session record, thereby changing the candidate hash. Capture the candidate **after** this transaction for the complete release dossier and obtain fresh review and activation signatures. `prepare`/`activate` must refuse an open session. No offline permit can be used after sealing or release preparation.

## Private evidence and independent qualification

Keep three separate results: **verified bytes**, **qualified observation**, **authorized native transition**. None implies the next. Private evidence hashes prove file identity/integrity relative to signed expectations, not provider truth, physical custody, complete source history, credential revocation or infrastructural fencing.

Proposed `verifyOfflineEvidence(candidatePath, envelope, manifest, loadCurrentTrust, qualificationVerifier, clock)` is read-only and returns a branded handle plus a redacted receipt. It first validates the offline signature domain/scope and current authority; verifies exact evidence-reference coverage; then validates each independently issued qualification against source interval, account/company/provider, region, task subject, observation time and outcome. Qualification verifier configuration is externally trusted/default-disabled, not loaded from the manifest. A report matching a hash or bearing an unauthenticated `qualified` field fails qualification. Unknown/ambiguous/contradictory outcomes can support a task that **records unresolved evidence only**; they cannot cancel a pending send, release a reservation, clear a claim or assert posting/non-posting.

Reuse the mechanics of `restore-evidence.ts` in a root-reviewed internal primitive, preserving the existing release verifier's semantics and tests. The current public function is coupled to a complete dossier and cannot be called unchanged for unresolved offline work. Required mechanics:

- Exact manifest fields `{version:1,root,files:[{reference,path}]}`, exact signed coverage without extras/conflicting digests, at most 1,000 files, 64 MiB/file, 256 MiB total; owner parsers may impose smaller bounds. Keep a bounded 64 KiB stream buffer and bounded parsed payload limits.
- Operator-owned private root/descendant directories and nonempty owner-only single-link regular files. Refuse symlinks, traversal, absolute child paths, hard links, duplicate paths/references, special files and permission drift. Root ancestors/storage must be independently trusted.
- Compare lstat/open-descriptor identity, uid/gid/mode/link count, size and nanosecond modification/change times before/during/after reading. Open no-follow/nonblocking. Retain/check parent directories; close descriptors on every outcome. Finish with a bounded fresh clock/trust/candidate/files check. Existing CLI control JSON reads are not this complete descriptor-bound boundary and must not be represented as one.
- Hash and parse the **same bytes**. Do not verify a path then independently reopen it to parse import facts. Parse through the verified descriptor/stream or bounded immutable copy under the same identity checks. No private bytes, filesystem paths, credentials or raw provider reports in database receipts, public docs, logs or errors. Retain only approved minimal normalized facts, necessary native external IDs and their hashes/references under the owner's data policy.
- Changed file/candidate, evidence growth, completion after expiry, clock rollback, registry replacement/revocation or incomplete coverage refuses the whole handle. Recheck pinned identities and qualifications immediately before the native transaction; a previous verification receipt is not a durable write permit. Preverify expensive streams outside the writer lock, then revalidate the pinned objects under the maintenance fence; if they cannot be kept immutable, reverify fully or refuse.

The current activation adapter consumes `RestoreControl` bound to a release, and its coordinator `observe` requires `reconciliation: "complete"`. Add a separate qualified offline observation/maintenance-lease interface for this contract; do not construct a pretend release or relax that activation predicate. Offline task qualification proves the exact task's admissibility and isolation while other dispositions may remain unresolved. Only the final seal/release checks demand complete all-owner reconciliation.

Source fence qualification must cover all source app/worker/CLI/callback writers and in-flight provider operations, plus delayed old control messages. Routing must remain isolated from customers/providers. Operations observation includes authenticated issuer/version, exact binding fields, observedAt/validUntil, nonreusable fence epoch, complete writer set, exclusive candidate maintenance writer identity, settled controls and external outcome coverage. No token expiry, missing lookup match, idle process or SQLite lock establishes external non-occurrence. Evidence acquisition/qualification is a separate authorized process; offline tasks never contact a provider.

## Fresh authority and commit sequence

The external registry must be authenticated, separately controlled and protected against rollback/replay; an old but valid signed registry is insufficient. Bind its revision/hash and re-read current revocations before evidence work, after evidence work, on transaction entry and immediately before commit. Use the existing fifteen-minute maximum as an upper bound for proposed task approval validity, not production freshness qualification. Operations and qualification expiry may be shorter: require `preparedAt <= now < min(all expiries)` and a nondecreasing clock at every boundary. Clock failure, unavailable current trust or unverifiable source lease closes this path.

IAM re-reads `currentActor`, organization, active status, current role, account restriction, security revision/password-change requirement and all affected site grants; use the owner's exact native permission checks. Apply required MFA/session checks if the chosen operator entry point uses sessions. External identity must be explicitly linked to the local executor, never matched by display name/email alone. Recovered IAM describes the backup date: it cannot establish current external finance/security/operations authority or bootstrap missing approval through a generic import. Missing identity reconciliation needs a separately designed IAM-owned recovery task; do not relax its checks here.

One new-effect execution has this order:

1. Strictly parse the allowlisted task/payload. Verify signed envelope, private evidence and qualification outside SQLite. Acquire a qualified, revocable maintenance lease bound to the exact task and recovery instance; this lease only permits the named local writer and keeps external IO fenced.
2. Root's dedicated dispatcher starts one synchronous native writer transaction. Refresh actor/trust/operations; assert phase/session CAS and same candidate file; compute the full candidate logical hash from the **same connection's transaction snapshot before any receipt/claim write**. The current `captureRestoreCandidate(path)` opens a separate read-only connection and is not a post-write in-transaction hashing API. Root must provide an internal read-only snapshot hash operation with identical typed canonical ordering/schema/sidecar rules, without granting owners unrestricted database access.
3. Compare native subject revision/state and copied claim identity. Owner proves task-specific external reference uniqueness and valid disposition. A copied provider claim is not this maintenance claim; do not clear it merely because it expired. Any retirement of that claim requires qualified settlement/fencing evidence and the owner's dedicated transition, retaining its original identity and outcome history.
4. Invoke the registered owner operation synchronously. The owner writes only its own append-only facts/projections and enforces its money/stock/custody invariants. Other owners are invoked through explicit transaction-scoped task methods, never foreign-table writes. If a required API opens another transaction or an effect crosses an unsupported owner boundary, implementation is blocked until root supplies the explicit composition.
5. Recheck phase/revision, IAM security, external trust/lease/clock and pinned evidence. Atomically persist owner result, consumed task binding, platform audit/idempotency receipt and session revision/lineage update. Preserve raw recovery hold and all prior releases. No provider callback, event consumer or asynchronous work runs inside/after this operation to finish missing facts.
6. Commit once, return a redacted receipt. Failure before commit rolls back all effects, claims, clocks and receipts. Crash during commit has an uncertain client result: recover by exact request lookup, never by inventing a new key. No background retry or provider resend.

Authority is required at the transaction's final authorization/commit boundary; revocation is not retroactive deletion of already committed facts. Qualified operations must serialize revocation/lease loss against this commit boundary (for example, a separately controlled local authority guard held through commit). Two timestamp samples alone cannot guarantee that an external revocation did not occur between them. Do not perform remote I/O while holding the SQLite transaction. If the configured deployment cannot provide that linearization/lease guarantee, report it as an infrastructure prerequisite and refuse production offline writes; synthetic guards qualify only application behavior.

Exact retry is a separate **read-only receipt recovery** branch: current identity/scope and raw generation/hold/phase rules still apply, and key+binding+payload must match the original immutable receipt and surviving owner effect. It must not compare the current candidate to the original pre-mutation hash as though it were a new write. Expired approval never authorizes another effect; a permitted scoped history read may return an already committed receipt explicitly as historical. Post-intent task retries refuse mutation and direct the caller to history inspection. Changed input/key scope conflicts and mismatched/missing owner evidence fail closed.

## Proposed durable storage and hash rules

Root must select the next schema version against the **current combined parent**, not assign schema 18 from this older schema-17 baseline. No DDL is implemented here. Suggested platform-owned records are: (a) recovery instance/session with region/hold tuple, revision, `open|sealed|abandoned`, complete release lineage and immutable begin/seal authorities; (b) append-only offline task receipts keyed permanently by `(instanceId,sessionId,owner,orgId,taskName,requestId)` with unique consumed binding, executor, native source/claim revisions, approvals, evidence/qualification/operations hashes, before-candidate hash and owner-result hash. Owners retain their own exact disposition/effect records and permanent native/provider reference reservations. Enforce a single open maintenance session per recovery instance and CAS revisions across connections. No persistent in-progress effect is necessary for a purely local atomic owner task; any separate maintenance-lease intent must remain conservatively recoverable on restart.

Include new session/receipt rows in candidate hashing. At present **only** `platform_restore_releases` rows are excluded, with its DDL still schema-bound. Do not expand exclusions to arbitrary owner records or new reconciliation history. Avoid self-referential hashes: a task receipt stores the full **pre-task** candidate hash and a digest of its normalized owning effect; it does not store a purported full post-state hash that includes itself. After committing, capture the full new candidate for the next task's fresh signed envelope. Opening/sealing a session also changes candidate bytes and invalidates preceding candidate signatures. Session CAS and consumed bindings prevent reuse; bytes hashes alone do not establish authority.

Restore must invalidate copied session/maintenance authority, rotate instance identity through the platform owner, preserve receipt/release history, and maintain provider refusal. Upgrades need frozen exact old profiles, fresh-file behavior, regional/reporting preservation, empty new tables and unchanged historical guards. Do not rewrite earlier version fingerprints or turn startup into implicit business repair.
